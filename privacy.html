Privacy Policy

How LotusTech Pty Ltd collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

LotusTech Pty Ltd (“LotusTech”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Information we collect

We only collect information you give us directly, for example when you:

  • send an enquiry or quote request through our website contact form;
  • register with us at a trade show or event, including through our booth sign-up page;
  • email or phone us, or do business with us as a customer or supplier.

This may include your name, job title, organisation, email address, phone number or WeChat ID, country or region, the products you are interested in, and any message you send us. We do not ask for sensitive information and ask that you do not send it to us.

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.

How we use your information

  • to respond to your enquiry and provide quotes, products and technical support;
  • to manage trade show registrations and hand out event gifts;
  • to send you information about our products, services and events where you have agreed to this;
  • to meet our legal, quality-system and record-keeping obligations.

You can opt out of marketing emails at any time by replying “unsubscribe” or emailing us at the address below.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our website host (GitHub Pages), our form processing service (Formspree), and Google Workspace / Google Sheets, which we use to record event registrations. These providers may store data on servers outside Australia, including in the United States. We take reasonable steps to make sure they protect your information. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Keeping your information secure

We store personal information in access-controlled accounts and limit access to staff who need it. We keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law, and then delete it.

Access, correction and removal

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within 30 days.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au.
